☽ The Tarot Oracle · Guide
What is Tarot?

Tarot is a set of 78 cards — each a mirror of the human experience. The 22 Major Arcana tell the story of the soul's journey; the 56 Minor Arcana map everyday life across four suits: Wands (fire · will), Cups (water · feeling), Swords (air · mind), and Pentacles (earth · body).

The cards don't predict a fixed future — they reveal energies, patterns, and possibilities already present in your situation. A reading is a conversation between your unconscious and the symbols on the cards.

How to Ask a Good Question
  • Open questions work best. "What energy surrounds my career right now?" reveals more than "Will I get the job?"
  • Use "I" language. Ask about your own energy and choices — not what others will do.
  • Be specific about the situation, not the desired answer. The cards respond to honesty.
  • No question is required. You can draw with an open mind and let the cards speak.
✦ "What do I need to know about this relationship?"  ·  "Where is my energy blocked?"  ·  "What is the lesson in this situation?"  ·  "What should I focus on this month?"
Creating the Right Space

Tarot works best when you slow down and arrive fully. You don't need candles or crystals — you need presence.

  • Take three slow breaths before you begin
  • Silence your phone; close other tabs
  • Hold your question in your mind — don't rush to type it. Let it settle
  • When the fan opens, pause. Notice which cards pull your attention
  • After the reading, sit with what arises — don't immediately explain it away
Choosing a Spread
  • Single Card — a daily focus, a quick answer, a yes/no
  • Three Card — past / present / future; situation / action / outcome
  • Love Cross — relationship dynamics between you and another person
  • Two Choices — when you're deciding between two clear paths
  • Horseshoe — a thorough 7-card view with hidden influences and advice
  • Celtic Cross — the classic 10-card deep dive; for complex situations
  • Tree of Life — Kabbalistic; for spiritual and life-purpose questions

The oracle will suggest a spread based on your question — you can always change it.
